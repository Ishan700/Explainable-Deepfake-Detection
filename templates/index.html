How a verdict is produced

  1. SampleUp to N frames are taken evenly across the clip - neighbouring frames are near-duplicates, so more adds little.
  2. DetectYuNet finds the largest face and five landmarks. The crop keeps a 30% margin, because face-swap seams live at the edge of the face.
  3. ClassifyEvery crop goes through an EfficientNet trained on real/fake faces. The video verdict is the mean P(fake) across frames.
  4. ExplainGrad-CAM highlights the pixels that drove the decision; the heatmap mass is attributed to eyes, mouth, nose, hairline, skin and the face boundary.
