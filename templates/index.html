How a verdict is reached

  1. Sample frames Up to 16 frames are taken evenly across the clip. Neighbouring frames are near-duplicates, so more adds little.
  2. Find the face A detector locates the largest face and five landmarks. The crop keeps a 30% margin because face-swap seams sit at the edge of the face.
  3. Score each frame Every crop goes through a CNN trained on real and manipulated faces. The verdict is the average probability across frames.
  4. Explain the decision Grad-CAM highlights the pixels that drove the score; that attention is mapped to eyes, mouth, nose, hairline, skin and the face boundary.
